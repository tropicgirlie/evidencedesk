# Credibility and revenue hypothesis

Reviewed 12 October 2026. This is a product hypothesis, not a forecast or legal opinion.

## Honest current position

Evidence Desk is credible as a portfolio example of careful product design, source linking, and cautious matching. A ten-notice historical index is too narrow to promise complete recall monitoring or to charge consumers for safety answers. Public trust depends on visible coverage limits, dates, corrections, and qualified review as the product grows.

## Where paid value might exist

Keep the public lookup free. Test a paid workflow with independent beauty retailers, salon groups, or small brand operations teams: map official recall identifiers to their own product catalog or inventory, keep an auditable notice trail, and help a staff member quickly find the original instructions. The paid value would be time saved in a recurring process, not access to public notices or a claim that a product is safe.

This is unvalidated. FDA already provides public recall notices and subscriptions, so customers would need to value the inventory matching and review workflow enough to pay for it. Do not sell broad monitoring until source coverage and refresh processes are demonstrably reliable.

## First validation steps

1. Interview five potential buyers about their current recall workflow, inventory identifiers, frequency, costs, and who approves a purchase.
2. Run a manual pilot with one partner and their own catalog, using source-linked notices and a documented review process.
3. Measure time saved, exact-match accuracy, false positives, missed notices, and whether the partner requests a repeat.
4. Offer a paid pilot only after the scope, editorial controls, and liability boundaries are clear.

## Trust rules

- Show where records came from, when they were reviewed, and what the collection excludes.
- Separate any sponsor or commercial relationship from the public findings. Do not let a brand pay to change a notice result.
- Use qualified legal and cosmetic-safety specialists before interpreting regulations, health effects, or litigation.
- Make correction requests and version history visible before broad promotion.
